Questions

What counts toward the free 500?

Only photos you confirm to delete. Reviewing and keeping are free and unlimited.

Doesn't Apple Photos already do this?

Photos merges exact duplicates. Kept helps with everything around them: similar shots, bursts, screenshots, blurry photos and long videos, one short round at a time.

Can I undo?

Before you confirm, always. After you confirm, photos are in Apple's Recently Deleted album, usually for up to 30 days. Apple can skip that step when iCloud storage is full, and Kept tells you when that applies.

Will it free up iCloud storage?

If iCloud Photos is on, deleting on one device deletes everywhere, which frees iCloud storage. Kept tells you before you confirm.

What does it cost?

Kept is free until you've cleared 500 photos. Then it's $5.99, once, through the App Store. No subscription and no ads.

Do my photos leave my phone?

No. Everything runs on your iPhone. There's no account and no upload. If you turn on place names for trips, Apple Maps receives an approximate location, never a photo.
